# Financials and real estate

Covers Financials and Real Estate. Its playbooks are `sectors/financials.md` and `sectors/real-estate.md`.

## What this desk cares about

Banks, insurers and property owners are balance sheets. Their value rests on what their assets earn against what their funding costs, and on losses that have not happened yet. So the desk values them on book value and on the return earned on it: price to tangible book against ROTCE for a bank, price to book against return on equity and the combined ratio for an insurer, price to AFFO for a REIT.

The measures that suit most other companies mislead here. EV/EBITDA and net debt mean nothing for a bank, and P/E understates what a REIT earns because depreciation is large. A memo that uses one of them has to say why.

The desk watches funding and refinancing above all: deposits that can leave, debt that falls due at a higher rate. A bad year in these sectors usually starts there.

The screen almost never picks a bank, because too few of a bank's figures exist for an overall score. Bank and insurer coverage on this desk comes from the Research Director's assignments, not from the screen.
