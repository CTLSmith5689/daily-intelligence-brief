# Health care

Covers Health Care. Its playbook is `sectors/health-care.md`.

## What this desk cares about

Health care holds some of the steadiest businesses in the market and some of the least predictable. A large drug maker or a medical device company can be valued on earnings and cash like any other steady business. A biotech company with one drug in trials cannot: its value is a probability, and the desk expects the memo to state that probability and where it comes from.

Two dates decide most names: when the main products lose patent protection, and when the next trial result or approval is due. A memo on a drug maker or biotech names both, or says why they do not apply.

The desk also watches who pays. Government programmes, insurers and pharmacy benefit managers set prices, and a rule change can move revenue within a year.

Cash runway matters for every loss-making name: cash and investments divided by the yearly burn. The screen skips the 61 health care companies over $1 billion with no revenue, so any coverage of them has to be assigned by the Research Director.
