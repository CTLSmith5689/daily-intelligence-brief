# Energy, materials and utilities

Covers Energy, Materials and Utilities. Its playbooks are `sectors/energy.md`, `sectors/materials.md` and `sectors/utilities.md`.

## What this desk cares about

Energy and materials companies mostly take the market price for what they sell, so the desk's first question is where that price sits against its own history, and what the company earns at a lower one. A memo names the price its base case assumes and the year of the company's own record its bear case resembles. A low P/E at a peak margin is the trap this desk sees most often.

Utilities are the opposite kind of business: regulated, slow and steady. The questions are how fast the rate base grows, what return the regulator allows, and how the capital plan is paid for.

What the three sectors share is capital. Each needs heavy spending to stay in business, so the desk judges every name on cash after that spending, and on how much debt it can carry through a bad year.

The pipeline has no commodity price series and no reserves, volume or rate base figures. Arguments that depend on them are made from the filing text, and the memo says where each figure came from.
