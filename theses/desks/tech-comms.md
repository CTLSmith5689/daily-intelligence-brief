# Technology and communications

Covers Information Technology and Communication Services. Its playbooks are `sectors/information-technology.md` and `sectors/communication-services.md`.

## What this desk cares about

Most of these companies are valued on growth that has not happened yet, so the desk's first question for any name is what the price requires: how many years of fast growth, at what margin. A memo that cannot say that in one or two sentences is not finished.

Across both sectors the desk asks whether growth pays for itself. Revenue that needs ever more sales spending, capital spending on networks, or spending on content is worth less than revenue that arrives with a rising margin. Free cash flow is the test, after counting stock-based pay as the cost it is.

Cycles matter more than the multiples suggest. Chip makers, hardware sellers and advertising businesses all have bad years, and the reported history shows how bad. A memo on one of them names the year its bear case resembles.

The desk's weak spot is data. The panel has no recurring revenue, backlog, subscriber or content-spending figures, and a third of the technology names have no usable P/E. Those arguments are made from the filings, and the memo says so.
