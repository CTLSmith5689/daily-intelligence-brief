 13. SEGMENT REPORTING The Company identifies any operating segments based on whether the Company’s Chief Executive Officer, who is the Chief Operating Decision Maker (“CODM”), regularly reviews operating results of particular components of the Company’s activities and allocates resources and assesses performance based on those results. The Company has one operating segment and, as a result, one reportable segment related to the sale of merchandise directly to clients who received a curated shipment of items or purchased directly from its website or mobile app. Financial information and data are provided to the CODM on a consolidated basis. The CODM uses consolidated net loss from continuing operations, as reported on the Company’s consolidated statements of operations, to evaluate performance, make key operating decisions, and allocate resources. Net loss from continuing operations is used to compare budget to actual results. All long-lived assets are located in the United States and revenue is attributable to customers based in the United States.
