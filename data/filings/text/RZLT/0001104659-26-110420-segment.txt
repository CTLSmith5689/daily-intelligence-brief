 NOTE 15 — SEGMENT DISCLOSURES The Company has determined that it operates as a single reportable segment which includes all of its activities as a clinical stage biopharmaceutical company. The CODM uses net loss as reported on the consolidated statement of operations to assess performance, analyze budget to actual results, forecast future operating results and cash requirements, and allocate resources for its single reportable segment. The significant segment expenses regularly reviewed by the CODM consist of clinical and manufacturing costs of ersodetug, personnel expenses, and other segment expenses. The measure of the operating segment assets is reported on the consolidated balance sheet as total assets and all of the Company's tangible assets are located in the United States.
